Privacy Policy

Last Updated: January 6, 2024

This Privacy Policy describes how Radiosh ("we," "our," or "us") handles information when you use our mobile application (the "App").

Important Summary: Radiosh does NOT collect, store, or share any personal data. The app operates entirely locally on your device.

1. Information Collection

No Personal Data Collected

Radiosh is designed with privacy as a core principle. The app:

  • Does not collect any personal information
  • Does not require user registration or accounts
  • Does not access your contacts, photos, or other personal files
  • Does not track your location
  • Does not use cookies or tracking technologies

2. Local Device Storage

The app may store certain preferences and settings locally on your device to enhance your user experience. This data:

  • Remains only on your device
  • Is never transmitted to our servers or third parties
  • Can be cleared by uninstalling the app

3. Third-Party Services

No Third-Party Tracking

Radiosh does not integrate with any third-party services that collect user information. Specifically:

  • No analytics services (Google Analytics, Firebase Analytics, etc.)
  • No advertising networks (AdMob, Facebook Ads, etc.)
  • No social media integrations
  • No data collection SDKs

4. Data Sharing

No Data Sharing with Third Parties

Since Radiosh does not collect any user data, there is no data to share with third parties. We do not sell, trade, or rent any user information because none is collected.

5. Permissions

If Radiosh requests any device permissions (such as network access for streaming radio stations), these permissions are used solely for:

  • Providing the core functionality of the app
  • Operating locally on your device
  • Enhancing user experience

No data accessed through permissions is collected or transmitted externally.

6. Children's Privacy

Radiosh is safe for users of all ages. Since we do not collect any personal data, we are fully compliant with children's privacy regulations including COPPA.

7. Data Security

While Radiosh doesn't collect or store your data externally, we encourage users to:

  • Keep their device's operating system updated
  • Use device security features (passcodes, biometrics)
  • Only install apps from trusted sources like Google Play Store

8. Changes to This Policy

If we ever change our privacy practices in the future, we will:

  • Update this Privacy Policy
  • Clearly indicate the changes
  • Update the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page

You are advised to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes.

9. Your Rights

Since Radiosh doesn't collect your data, you don't need to exercise data rights such as access, correction, or deletion. Your privacy is protected by design.
